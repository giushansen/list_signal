## Differentiators (one line each, reuse anywhere)

- **Discovers, not just enriches.** Companies are found from Certificate
  Transparency logs within hours of launch, not scraped from filings.
- **One record, every signal.** Tech stack, revenue, employees, hiring,
  SEO, and contacts on the same row, instead of across three vendors.
- **Flat pricing, no credits.** $39 and $149, where comparable tools start
  at $250 to $295.
- **Built for AI agents.** REST API plus an MCP server in the official
  registry, with a free tier of 1,000 calls a month.

## Honest limits (say these; they build trust and preempt bad reviews)

- Company and domain level, not a personal-contact database. For
  decision-maker emails and direct dials, Apollo or ZoomInfo are better,
  and most teams use ListSignal to pick the accounts and a contact tool to
  reach them.
- Revenue and employee figures are modelled estimates with a published
  method, not filed accounts.
- Deepest on ecommerce and SaaS; thinner on offline/local businesses.

# Site-by-site

## 1. AlternativeTo (highest value, ~30 min)

The point is not the listing, it is appearing **on competitor pages**.

1. Sign up at alternativeto.net.
2. Add the app: alternativeto.net/manage/add-app/
   - Name: ListSignal, URL: https://listsignal.com
   - Description: the 50-word version above
   - Licence: Commercial, Freemium. Platforms: Online / Web / SaaS
   - Categories: Business & Commerce, Office & Productivity
   - Tags: as above
3. **Then the real work.** Visit each competitor page and add ListSignal
   as an alternative, with a one-line reason:
   - alternativeto.net/software/builtwith/ - "Same tech detection plus
     revenue, hiring and contacts, from $39/mo instead of $295."
   - alternativeto.net/software/wappalyzer/ - "Tech lookup with company
     data attached, flat pricing, no expiring credits."
   - alternativeto.net/software/storeleads/ - "Shopify depth without the
     $250 floor, and it covers SaaS and agencies too."
   - alternativeto.net/software/apollo-io/ - "Finds the accounts Apollo
     cannot see; pairs with it rather than replacing it."
4. Ask two or three people to "Like" the entry on those pages. Position in
   the alternatives list is vote-driven.

## 2. SaaSHub (~20 min)

Its auto-generated "X alternatives" pages rank, so a dense profile matters.

1. Submit at saashub.com/submit
2. Fill **everything**: description (100-word), pricing (Free / $39 / $149),
   all features, logo, screenshots, social links.
3. In the competitors/alternatives field, explicitly name: BuiltWith,
   Wappalyzer, Store Leads, Apollo, ZoomInfo, Clay.
4. Categories: Sales Intelligence, Lead Generation, Market Research.

## 3. Crunchbase (~20 min)

Credibility and brand-name search, not traffic.

1. crunchbase.com/add-new, create the organization.
2. Legal name **Keybloc Pte Ltd**, brand **ListSignal**, HQ Singapore.
3. Categories: Sales Automation, Lead Generation, Market Research,
   Database, SaaS, Business Intelligence.
4. Description: 100-word version. Add founder profile and LinkedIn.
5. Funding: "Bootstrapped" is a legitimate and credible answer.

## 4. StackShare (~15 min)

The audience is people who care about tech stacks, which is the product.

1. stackshare.io - search "ListSignal"; claim it if present, else submit
   at stackshare.io/vendors.
2. Category: Business Intelligence / Analytics.
3. Lead with the meta-angle: a tool that detects 293 technologies across
   24M sites, listed on the site about technology choices.
4. Also publish ListSignal's **own** stack (Elixir, Phoenix, ClickHouse) -
   it earns a second profile and engineers find it credible.

## 5. G2 and Capterra (list now, reviews over the month)

Free to list. They rank nothing until reviews land, and AI assistants cite
both heavily.

1. **Capterra first**: capterra.com/vendors. One listing syndicates to
   GetApp and Software Advice, so it is three for the price of one.
   Categories: Lead Generation, Sales Intelligence, Market Research.
2. **G2 second**: g2.com/products/new. Categories: Sales Intelligence,
   Lead Intelligence, Market Intelligence.
3. Thresholds that matter: Capterra needs 5 reviews for category
   inclusion, 20+ at 4.0 for a Shortlist. G2 needs 10 in a rolling 12
   months to appear on the Grid.
4. Getting them: ask your happiest users directly, right after a win (a
   good export, a reply from a prospect). Send a one-click review link.
   G2's disclosed gift-card program is allowed and roughly doubles
   response. Concentrate on ONE platform until it crosses the threshold;
   five reviews spread across five sites reach nothing.

## Order of work

Day one: AlternativeTo (including the four competitor pages) and SaaSHub.
Day two: Crunchbase and StackShare, then claim the Capterra and G2
profiles. Then the review push, one platform at a time.
